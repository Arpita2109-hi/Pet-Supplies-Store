HAPPY PAWS - DIGITAL BANKING DEMO

What was changed:
- Cash on Delivery was removed from checkout.
- Fake card checkout was removed.
- Checkout now uses Happy Paws Bank only.
- bank_payment.php verifies account number + 4-digit PIN.
- Successful payment deducts the amount from bank_accounts.balance.
- Every payment attempt is stored in bank_transactions.
- orders.payment_status becomes 'paid' after successful payment.
- orders.transaction_id stores the successful bank transaction ID.
- transaction_history.php shows the logged-in customer's bank payment history.
- Admin Manage Orders now shows payment status and transaction ID.

Database expected:
- bank_accounts
- bank_transactions
- orders with payment_status and transaction_id columns
